How to read the archive

Records first. Claims with limits.

The transcript preserves what was recorded. Analysis summarizes patterns in those records. Fieldnotes trace selected claims to source spans. Reports of actions, links, acknowledgements, and repeated summaries are evidence of communication; they do not independently prove external outcomes or influence.
